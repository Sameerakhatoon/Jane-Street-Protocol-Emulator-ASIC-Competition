# SPI targets. Between two bytes the lane drives the next MISO bit, tests cs_n, pushes and jumps
# back to the byte loop, past its wait for SCK low (the byte's last wait saw it; going back to
# that wait missed the next rise and held these targets to 2.13 MHz, 1.78 MHz at 50 MHz). At
# 4 MHz (3.5 MHz at 50 MHz) the next rise can still come first and the first bit of the next
# byte is sampled up to 7 cycles after it, at the next fall (R311 shift mosi late). Back to back
# they build up to 3.32 MHz at 60 MHz, 2.77 MHz at 50 MHz (9 cycles a half period, any pads);
# 4 MHz needs SCK low >= 184 ns between bytes (167 ns on the whole pad path), which no rule here
# can state; at 50 MHz 4 MHz does not build at all, 3.5 MHz needs 220 ns (200 ns).
# With bytes back to back the first edge after CS also needs t_css >= 117 ns (the lane drives MISO and sets its counter first),
# plus the 25 ns pad skew between CS and SCK: 142 ns. With 117 ns the reference lane misses the
# first rise when CS comes through its pad 25 ns slower than SCK.
